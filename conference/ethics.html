SMAIL Conference, First Edition 2027

Ethics

The standards the review process and the conference itself are held to.

Review integrity

Double-blind peer review by at least three reviewers per full paper, with conflict-of-interest management built into reviewer assignment.

Data governance & disclosure

Authors must disclose ethics approvals and data governance for any clinical or health data used in their submission.

Responsible publication

Priority is given to reproducibility and clinically meaningful evaluation over benchmark performance alone.

Misconduct & dual submission

The conference follows standard academic norms on plagiarism and dual submission.

A fully detailed policy will be published.
