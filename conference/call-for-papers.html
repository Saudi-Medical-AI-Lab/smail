Call for Papers

Seven research tracks, four paper formats, and a double-blind review process built for clinically meaningful, reproducible medical AI research.

Scientific Scope

Seven research tracks

Clinical ML & Risk Prediction

Automated diagnosis, disease-course prognosis, clinical decision support, risk-sharing models.

AI in Medical Imaging

Radiology, neuroimaging, cardiac and retinal imaging, multimodal imaging.

Generative & Foundation Models, Medical NLP

Large medical language models, multimodal vision models, healthcare AI agents, Arabic and English clinical text.

Multimodal Digital Health

Electronic health records, wearables, remote monitoring, digital mental health.

Trustworthy & Responsible Medical AI

Fairness, bias, interpretability, privacy, governance.

Translational & Real-World Applications

External validation, real-world deployment, health economics.

Genomics, Drug Discovery & Computational Biology

Genome analysis, molecular property prediction, drug discovery and repurposing, multi-omics models.

Paper Formats

Four ways to submit

Full papers are the primary submission track; the other formats broaden participation.

Full Paper

The primary track. Accepted papers require a print-ready camera-ready copy.

Short Paper

4–6 pages, for a more compact contribution.

Optional category, pending committee decision.

Poster

A juried track for applied or early-stage work, presented in the poster session.

Demo

Live or recorded demonstrations of deployed or deployable medical AI systems.

Best Paper Award for Saudi & MENA Data

A dedicated award for the strongest accepted paper built on Saudi or wider MENA regional data.

Review Process

How papers are reviewed

Submissions go through OpenReview.

  • Double-blind review by at least three reviewers per full paper.
  • Reviewers assess novelty, methodological rigor, clinical significance, and evaluation & reproducibility.
  • Authors must disclose ethics approvals and data governance for any clinical or health data used.
  • Accepted papers are scheduled for an oral slot or the poster & demo session.
Publication

Published with a permanent DOI

Accepted papers are published as open-access proceedings on smail.org.sa, with a permanent, citable DOI for every paper, registered through Crossref.

  • Every accepted paper gets its own page on smail.org.sa and a permanent DOI.
  • Indexed with major research databases, including Google Scholar and Semantic Scholar.
  • Proceedings become publicly available within 30 days of the conference.
  • Accepted full papers are prepared in a unified LaTeX format for the camera-ready version.
