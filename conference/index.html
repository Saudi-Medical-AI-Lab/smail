About the Conference

Medical AI research, given a rigorous stage

The conference invites research papers from students and researchers in medical AI, reviewed in multiple stages before the strongest work is presented over two days. It aims to become a leading Saudi venue for researchers from the Kingdom and the wider MENA region.

For the Kingdom, it's a chance to strengthen the medical-AI research ecosystem from the ground up.

SDAIA

Under the supervision of the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA)

Expected Impact

Why this conference matters

Four ways the conference aims to move the needle for Saudi medical AI research.

A Real Publication Platform

Genuine peer review and publication experience for early-career researchers and students.

Bridges Academia & Healthcare

Connects universities with the Ministry of Health, hospitals, and Saudi health-tech companies.

Aligned with Vision 2030

Supports the Kingdom's digital health transformation and the localization of AI capabilities.

Regional Research Leadership

Aims to make the Kingdom a regional hub for specialized scientific events, and opens collaboration with Gulf and Arab academic institutions.

Scientific Scope

Seven research tracks

Full submission details on the Call for Papers page.

Clinical ML & Risk Prediction

Automated diagnosis, disease-course prognosis, clinical decision support, risk-sharing models.

AI in Medical Imaging

Radiology, neuroimaging, cardiac and retinal imaging, multimodal imaging.

Generative & Foundation Models, Medical NLP

Large medical language models, multimodal vision models, healthcare AI agents, Arabic and English clinical text.

Multimodal Digital Health

Electronic health records, wearables, remote monitoring, digital mental health.

Trustworthy & Responsible Medical AI

Fairness, bias, interpretability, privacy, governance.

Translational & Real-World Applications

External validation, real-world deployment, health economics.

Genomics, Drug Discovery & Computational Biology

Genome analysis, molecular property prediction, drug discovery and repurposing, multi-omics models.
